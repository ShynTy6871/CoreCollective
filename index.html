For Military Families

Military & PCS Relocation

A PCS move runs on a different timeline than a typical home search. Our team helps you move efficiently, without cutting corners.

Permanent Change of Station orders often compress months of house-hunting into a single trip. Marsha Watson, our team lead, holds a Military Relocation Professional (MRP) certification and works specifically with service members navigating this exact timeline — coordinating showings around leave schedules, pre-screening listings before you arrive, and helping you move quickly without skipping the diligence that protects you.

Our service area includes Goldsboro, home to Seymour Johnson Air Force Base and the 4th Fighter Wing — real, local experience with the base community, not just a general familiarity with military moves.

We help eligible buyers understand VA loan benefits specifically: no down payment requirement, no private mortgage insurance, and guidance on rolling the funding fee into your loan. Read our full PCS move guide on the blog for a step-by-step breakdown.
